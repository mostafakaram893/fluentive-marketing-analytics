# 📈 Fluentive — Digital Marketing & Lead Conversion Intelligence Dashboard

An executive digital marketing performance and lead generation analytics dashboard developed using **Microsoft Power BI** and **DAX**. This project models end-to-end sales funnel performance, evaluating multi-channel campaign ROI, lead acquisition costs, and sales team conversion efficiency.

---

## 🎯 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- **Total Leads:** Aggregate volume of incoming marketing leads across all active channels.
- **Total Converted:** Number of successfully converted paying clients.
- **Conversion Rate (%):** Lead-to-customer conversion efficiency.
- **Cost Per Lead (CPL):** Marketing acquisition spend efficiency per acquired lead.
- **Total Budget:** Total capital allocated across social advertising campaigns.

---

## 📊 Dashboard Visualizations & Insights

- **Lead Status Breakdown (Donut/Pie Chart):** Real-time segmentation by status (`Converted`, `Follow Up`, `Not Interested`).
- **Sales Representative Performance (Bar Chart):** Tracking lead handling volume and conversion ratios per sales rep.
- **Platform Acquisition (Clustered Bar Chart):** Comparative acquisition volume between **Instagram (IG)** and **Facebook (FB)**.
- **Campaign Performance (Column Chart):** Comparing total leads versus conversions by individual campaign.
- **Interactive Multi-Level Slicers:** Dynamic slicing by Campaign Name, Date, Study Level, and Delivery Mode (Online vs. Offline).

---

## 🛠 Tech Stack

- **BI Tool:** Microsoft Power BI Desktop
- **Data Modeling:** Power Query (ETL), DAX measures
- **Data Source:** CRM Lead Tracking & Campaign Performance Datasets
